Travelling for Hajj or Umrah from **Dubai, UAE** is a blessing - and a logistics exercise. These five tips come from decades of walking with families from briefing to the gates of Makkah.

### 1. Confirm documents early
Passport validity, photos, and vaccination certificates should be ready well before departure. Our Dubai team will guide you through each requirement.

### 2. Keep copies in two places
Passport pouch on your person; photocopies and phone scans with a trusted family member. Share your group leader’s number before you leave Dubai airport.

### 3. Travel light, pack smart
One cabin bag for essentials; check airline rules for liquids and medicines. Label everything with your group name.

### 4. Pack for Dubai heat *and* Madinah evenings
Breathable clothing for transit, and a light layer for cooler nights in Madinah. Comfortable sandals matter more than fashion.

### 5. Ask every question before you fly
