## Begin with calm preparation

Travelling for Hajj or Umrah from **Chennai** is a blessing - and a logistics exercise. These five tips come from decades of walking with families through Kilpauk briefings to the gates of Makkah.

### 1. Attend the pre-departure briefing

We cover Ihram rules, group meeting points, and emergency contacts. Bring every adult traveller if possible. Children remember more than you think when they hear the plan once.

### 2. Keep documents in two places

Passport pouch on your person; photocopies and phone scans with a trusted family member. Share your group leader’s number before you leave Chennai airport.

### 3. Pace yourself in the Haram

The first Tawaf can feel overwhelming. Rest, hydrate, and follow your guide’s timing. There is barakah in steady worship - not in rushing every gate photo.

### 4. Pack for Chennai heat *and* Madinah evenings

Light cotton for daytime; a modest layer for air-conditioned coaches and cooler nights. Comfortable footwear matters more than new pairs that blister.

### 5. Stay connected to your group - and to dua

Agree a simple family meeting point. Between rites, make dua for those who could not travel. Many pilgrims tell us this becomes the most memorable part of the journey.
