## Two sacred journeys, one purpose

Both **Umrah** and **Hajj** draw the believer closer to Allah. Understanding the difference helps families plan dates, budgets, and expectations - especially first-time pilgrims from India and the UAE.

## Obligation & timing

- **Hajj** is one of the five pillars of Islam and is obligatory once in a lifetime for those who are physically and financially able. It takes place only in **Dhul Hijjah**.  
- **Umrah** is a highly rewarded Sunnah that can be performed **any time of the year**, subject to visa and travel arrangements.

## Core rites at a glance

| | Hajj | Umrah |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ihram | Yes | Yes |
| Tawaf & Sa’i | Yes | Yes |
| Arafat, Muzdalifah, Mina | Required | Not part of Umrah |
| Duration | Multi-day rites within the Hajj season | Often completed in hours, stay can be longer |
