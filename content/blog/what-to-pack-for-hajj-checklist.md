## Start with intention, then pack with care

Hajj is a journey of the heart - but thoughtful packing makes the days of worship smoother. Families travelling with ELITE ALHUSSAM from **Dubai, UAE** often ask what is truly essential. Use this checklist as a calm starting point. **Haj services are for Indian passport holders only.**

## Documents & essentials

- Valid Indian passport, visas, and printed copies in a waterproof pouch  
- Vaccination records and any hospital letters your doctor recommends  
- Hotel and package vouchers (keep digital copies on your phone too)  
- Emergency contacts: family, and your ELITE ALHUSSAM group leader  

## Clothing & Ihram

- Ihram garments for men (and spare set)  
- Comfortable abayas / modest clothing for women, with layers for AC buses and evening chill  
- Soft sandals that stay on during Tawaf and Sa’i  
- Light socks, undergarments, and a small laundry bag  

## Health & comfort

- Personal medicines (labelled), ORS sachets, and blister plasters  
- Unscented moisturiser, lip balm, and sunscreen suitable for Ihram rules where relevant  
- Refillable water bottle and a compact folding fan  
- Eye mask and earplugs for rest between rites  

> Tip from our guides: pack light. You will walk more than you expect - every extra kilo becomes a distraction from Ibadah.

## Spiritual kit

- Pocket Qur’an or dua book  
- Tasbih / digital counter  
- Small notebook for reflections
